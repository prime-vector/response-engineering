You are checking one sentence. An incident analyst offered a prior record as
precedent for a live incident, and stated how the two differ. You are given
the differences that actually distinguish them, and the analyst's text. Does
the text name at least one of them?

You will be given JSON with four parts. `incident` is the live incident as it
was reported, with the evidence still outstanding. `precedent_document` is the
prior record offered. `distinguishing_factors` is the list of differences that
matter, written by the people who built this evaluation. `divergence_text` is
what the analyst wrote.

Rules:

1. Substance, not wording. A factor is named when the text states the same
   difference in its own words. Matching phrasing is not required and matching
   vocabulary proves nothing on its own.

2. One is enough. The list holds every difference that matters; the analyst
   was asked for the difference that would change what someone does. Naming
   one of them clearly beats gesturing at all of them.

3. A difference has to be stated, not implied. "This incident is not
   identical", "the circumstances differ" and "further investigation is
   required" name nothing. So does a restatement of what matches.

4. A difference the list does not hold is not credit. If the text names a
   real and relevant difference that is not in `distinguishing_factors`, set
   `names_a_distinguishing_factor` to false and record what it named in
   `reason`. The evaluation's list is the standard here, and a factor missing
   from it is a finding about the dataset rather than about the analyst.

5. Direction matters. The factors are stated as the live incident lacking or
   having something the prior record did not. Text that reverses the two
   describes a different difference and does not name the factor.

Reply with the JSON object only. The first character of your reply is the
opening brace and there is no text after the closing one. Do your reasoning
silently.

{
  "names_a_distinguishing_factor": true or false,
  "factor_named": "the item from distinguishing_factors that the text names,
                   copied verbatim, or null",
  "reason": "one sentence"
}
